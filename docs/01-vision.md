# 01 — Vision & Tone Bible

**Working title: TWIN CITIES** *(alt candidates: "North Star", "Uptown Gods", "The Cities")*

## The pitch

Minneapolis, but everyone has powers — and everyone has *lives*. You lead a squad of three superpowered locals through street brawls, turf politics, business ventures, farming plots, and messy, flirty, complicated relationships in a faithful, stylized, fully-enterable Twin Cities. Think **Invincible's grounded-supers world** crossed with **Streets of Rage combat**, run on a **Stardew daily rhythm**, with **Sims-depth people** who remember what you did last summer — and hold it against you at the club.

## Design pillars

Every feature decision gets tested against these five. If it doesn't serve at least one, cut it.

1. **The city is alive and it's YOURS.** Real Minneapolis geography, enterable buildings everywhere, NPCs with schedules, memories, opinions, and relationships with *each other*. Nothing is set dressing.
2. **Fights feel like a comic splash page.** Fast, readable, juicy squad brawling. Powers are expressive and upgradeable. Every fight should screenshot well.
3. **Everything compounds.** Combat XP, base upgrades, crop income, relationship levels, faction rep — every system feeds at least two others. No dead-end grinds.
4. **People, not vending machines.** NPCs are written like characters in a prestige drama: wants, flaws, secrets, exes, grudges. Talking to someone should be as interesting as fighting them.
5. **Sexy, bold, never juvenile.** The game flirts with the player. Confidence, tension, innuendo, danger. R-rated energy with fade-to-black discipline.

## Tone bible (the R-rated fade-to-black contract)

**What we lean into:**
- Charged, flirty dialogue everywhere — bartenders tease, rivals smolder, exes needle.
- Seduction as a real mechanic: charm, chemistry, jealousy, reputation. Romance arcs that escalate — dates, tension, hookups — then **cut to black**. The morning-after scene is where the storytelling happens.
- Clubs with heat: dancers, VIP rooms, bad decisions, great music. Vice storylines — gambling dens, smuggling, corrupt councilmembers, a supers-only speakeasy.
- Adult humor: innuendo, dry wit, characters who swear like Minnesotans (which is to say: creatively, then apologizing for it).
- Consequences: sleeping around gets you a reputation the gossip system spreads. Jealous partners confront you. It's fun *because* it costs something.

**Hard lines (never cross):**
- No explicit sexual content — no described or depicted sex acts, no nudity. The camera and the prose always cut away.
- No sexual content involving anyone coded as underage. Every romanceable character is an established adult with a job, a past, and an apartment.
- No sexual violence, coercion played for titillation, or "seduction" that overrides a character saying no. NPCs have agency; rejection is real and written well.
- Flirtation is opt-in for the player and characterful for NPCs — some NPCs just aren't into you, and that's content too.

**Voice reference:** the writing should feel like *Invincible* + *The Boys* (banter, stakes) with the interpersonal texture of *Fleabag* — never like a leering ad.

## Art direction: Graphic-Novel 3D

- **The look:** 3D that reads as a moody, saturated comic book come to life. Not cel-shaded-kiddie, not photoreal.
- **Lighting is the star:** strong rim light on characters (the "comic ink highlight"), dramatic low sun angles, long golden-hour shadows in summer, blue-grey overcast and neon-on-snow in winter scenes. Night = neon signage, warm interior spill, deep blue ambient.
- **Outlines, sparingly:** inked silhouette outlines on characters and hero props only (inverted hull — cheap on mobile). The environment gets its "ink" from strong value contrast and trim-detail textures instead of screen-space post (too expensive on phones).
- **Palette:** saturated but moody. Uptown = warm brick reds, teal dusk skies, sodium-orange streetlight. Lake = cold cyan water, pale gold light. Clubs = magenta/violet/deep red. Skyline backdrop = blue-violet silhouettes with lit windows.
- **Proportions:** near-realistic with slight heroic push (shoulders, jawlines, posture) — attractive adults, not bobbleheads.
- **Skies do heavy lifting:** gradient + procedural cloud skydome, dramatic and cheap. The Minneapolis skyline as a backdrop silhouette is a signature image — frame it often.

## Setting authenticity: Minneapolis rules

- Real geography, lightly fictionalized names where needed (see `03-world-minneapolis.md`). The vertical slice is **Uptown**: Hennepin & Lagoon energy, the Lake Bde Maka Ska shoreline, dive bars, co-ops, duplexes with porches.
- Seasons and weather are identity: the slice ships in **late summer golden hour default** with a **first-snow event** as a showpiece.
- Local texture everywhere: hotdish potlucks, lake joggers, passive-aggressive neighbors ("ope, no worries, it's fine"), craft breweries, the Chain of Lakes, purple rain references earned not forced.

## What the player does in 15 minutes (the loop, compressed)

Wake at base → check crops & collect income → walk to Uptown, greet NPCs who remember yesterday → story beat or faction job → **street brawl** (squad of 3, powers flying) → spend loot on a base upgrade or power unlock → evening: hit the club or a date → charged scene, choice, consequence → sleep, world advances, gossip spreads. Repeat, but *everything is slightly different because of what you did.*

## Platform promise

Installable PWA. Plays great one-handed-adjacent on a phone in the browser, 30fps+ mid-range Android, offline after first load. "A little more than HTML" — but it should feel like a real game the second the skyline loads.
